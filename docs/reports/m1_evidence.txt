Interpretation
==============================================================================
  C1/C2: r_s = 2GM/c^2 reproduces published radii to <1e-4 relative.
  C3:    the closed form R_krit = sqrt(3c^2/(8 pi G rho)) agrees with an
         independent bisection root of r_s(M(R)) - R = 0.
  C4:    the correct angular term r*f carries the factor f = 1 - rs/r;
         omitting it (as the reference 3D/GPU code does) reduces the
         quadratic term coefficient from 3/2 to 1/2 and therefore
         under-predicts the deflection by a factor approaching 1/3, a
         defect that vanishes only in the far-field limit.
         The correct branch reproduces 2*rs/b, with a residual that
         falls off as the analytic next-order term 15 pi rs/(32 b) -
         i.e. the agreement improves exactly as the expansion requires.
