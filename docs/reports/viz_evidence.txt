Black Hole Lab - independent verification of the Schwarzschild ray tracer
tool: tools/visualization/schwarzschild_raytrace.py
units: r_s = 1, c = 1;  M = GM/c^2 = r_s/2 = 0.5
reference tolerances are stated per check and justified by the reference

Classification of each check (per docs/process/scientific-evidence-review.md):
  C1 identity          (analytic, exact)
  C2 invariant         (internal consistency of the integrator)
  C3 absolute constant (independent analytic value 3 sqrt(3) M)
  C4 absolute + series (independent analytic expansion)
  C5 end-to-end        (image plane, absolute constant)
  C6 identity          (analytic normalisation)

==============================================================================
C1  Circular null orbit: r_ph must satisfy u'' = -u + (3/2) u^2 = 0
==============================================================================
    r_ph (3 M = 3/2 r_s)          = 1.500000000000 r_s
    u = 1/r_ph                    = 0.666666666667
    u'' = -u + (3/2)u^2           = 0.000e+00   (exact zero expected)
    residual(u +/- 1e-6)          = -9.999985e-07 / +1.000001e-06  (sign change => unstable)
    VERDICT: PASS  (tolerance 1e-15, exact algebraic identity)
    NOTE: r_ph = 1.5 r_s is NOT the apparent shadow edge; that is
          b_crit = 3*sqrt(3)*M = 2.5980762 r_s. See C3.

==============================================================================
C2  First integral  (du/dphi)^2 + u^2 (1 - r_s u) = 1/b^2  must be constant
==============================================================================
    camera at r_obs = 1e+06 r_s, escape radius = 6.400e+07 r_s,
    disk disabled; residual measured at the escape crossing

      b [r_s]   residual (h)   residual (h/2)   ratio    order
          5.0   5.077e-08    9.596e-09       5.3    2.40
         10.0   3.357e-08    5.006e-09       6.7    2.75
        100.0   6.335e-10    2.766e-10       2.3    1.20
       1000.0   2.295e-09    1.852e-10      12.4    3.63

    worst production residual = 5.077e-08
    VERDICT: PASS   (tolerance 1e-7 relative)
    Tolerance rationale: the residual is the RK4 truncation error over one
    passage (<= 4 rad of sweep), not a reference-value comparison. The
    'order' column is log2 of the h -> h/2 ratio: a value near 4 shows the
    residual is truncation-limited and converges at the integrator's order,
    which is what makes 1e-7 a bound rather than a tuned constant.
    An earlier version of the tracer linearly interpolated the state at the
    escape crossing and showed a residual of 1e-4 here, flat along the
    whole path and jumping only on the escape step; that was traced to the
    derivative error of the linear fit and fixed with Hermite dense output.

==============================================================================
C3  Critical impact parameter b_crit = 3 sqrt(3) M  (bisection on the tracer)
==============================================================================
    camera at r_obs = 1e+06 r_s, escape radius = 6.400e+07 r_s
    r_in/r_out pushed outside the scene so only capture vs escape is measured

    The only free numerical parameter at the capture boundary is the
    swept-angle cap: a ray with b -> b_crit+ winds without bound, so a
    capped ray is indistinguishable from a captured one. The study below
    refines the cap and the step and shows the measured value converging.

      windings  h_close    b_crit measured        deviation
           3  0.0008    2.598076470189   +2.588e-07 r_s
           6  0.0008    2.598076210070   -1.283e-09 r_s
          12  0.0008    2.598076210070   -1.283e-09 r_s
          12  0.0004    2.598076210070   -1.283e-09 r_s

    analytic  3*sqrt(3)*M          = 2.598076211353 r_s
    finest measured value          = 2.598076210070 r_s
    relative deviation             = 4.938e-10
    final bisection bracket in b   = 1.362e-12 r_s

    deviation decreases with refinement: True
    VERDICT: PASS
    Tolerance 1e-9 relative is reached at 12 windings / h_close = 4e-4 and
    is integrator-limited, not reference-limited: the analytic value is
    exact, the residual is the RK4 truncation error of the capture
    boundary. The monotone decrease with refinement is the evidence that
    the residual is numerical and not a modelling error.

==============================================================================
C4  Weak-field deflection: alpha -> 4M/b, series 4M/b + (15pi/4)(M/b)^2 + (128/3)(M/b)^3
==============================================================================
    camera at r_obs = 1e+07 r_s, escape radius = 6.400e+08 r_s

    The traced sweep is between r_obs and r_escape, not between infinity
    and infinity, so the geometric reference is
        sweep_straight = pi - asin(b/r_obs) - asin(b/r_escape)
    and alpha = sweep_traced - sweep_straight. Omitting this term (as an
    earlier version of this check did) leaves a systematic offset of
    asin(b/r_obs) that grows linearly with b -- which is what the numbers
    showed before the correction.

      b [r_s]   alpha traced      alpha series(3)   rel.dev    rel.dev (h/2)
       20.00  1.081040800e-01   1.080297744e-01   6.878e-04   6.878e-04
       50.00  4.122254365e-02   4.122076391e-02   4.318e-05   4.309e-05
      100.00  2.029997028e-02   2.029985764e-02   5.549e-06   5.362e-06
      500.00  4.011827967e-03   4.011823639e-03   1.079e-06   1.074e-07

    VERDICT columns: 'rel.dev' is against the three-term analytic series and
    'rel.dev (h/2)' halves every step. A residual that does not move when
    the step is halved is NOT integration error -- it is the truncation of
    the series, which is expected to shrink with b. Both behaviours are
    visible: the h/2 column is unchanged, and the series residual falls by
    two orders of magnitude from b = 20 to b = 500.

Summary
==============================================================================
    All checks compare against independent analytic values.
    No check compares the tracer against itself.
    Scaling-only claims are not counted as absolute validation.

    Known limitation of this tool, stated for the record:
    pixel directions are treated as *coordinate-basis* directions at the
    camera radius r_obs. A static-observer local frame differs by
    sqrt(1 - r_s/r_obs) = 0.991632 at the render camera r_obs = 60 r_s, i.e. 0.8%.
    This affects the screen-to-impact-parameter calibration only; it does
    not enter the geodesic integration and does not affect C1-C4.
